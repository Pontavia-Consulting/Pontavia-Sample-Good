About Fern & Field

A small nursery with a simple idea: plant what already wants to grow here.

Started on a horse farm

Dana Whitfield grew up off Highway 326 watching her family fight the same losing battle every summer: thirsty grass, brown patches, and a water bill that kept climbing.

After 12 years at a wholesale native nursery, she opened Fern & Field in 2014 to help homeowners do it differently. Today our team of four designs, plants and cares for yards all over Marion County.

We still grow most of our natives on site, and we will always tell you when the cheaper option is the right one.

What we believe

Right plant, right place

A plant that suits your soil and sun needs less water, less spraying and less of your weekend.

Straight answers

Written quotes, flat prices, and a one-year guarantee on every plant we install.

Good for wildlife

Native plants feed the butterflies, bees and birds that make a yard feel alive.
