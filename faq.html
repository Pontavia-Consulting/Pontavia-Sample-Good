Questions we hear a lot

If yours isn't here, call us or send a message. A real person answers.

Do you deliver?

Yes, anywhere in Marion County. Delivery is free on orders over $150 within 15 miles of the nursery.

Will native plants really survive without watering?

After their first summer, most will. We water them in at planting and give you a simple schedule for the first 12 weeks.

Do you guarantee your plants?

Every plant we install is guaranteed for one year. Plants bought at the nursery are guaranteed for 30 days.

Can I bring a photo of my yard?

Please do. Photos of each side of the house, plus a rough idea of where the sun hits, make a quick visit much more useful.

Do you work with HOAs?

All the time. We can draw your plan to match your HOA's approval form and plant list.

When is the best time to plant in Ocala?

October through March for trees and shrubs. Most perennials can go in any time if you water them through the first few weeks.
